# Fonts

`montserrat-latin.woff2` and `montserrat-latin-italic.woff2` are Montserrat, by Julieta Ulanovsky
and the Montserrat Project Authors, from Google Fonts: the Latin subset of its variable font,
weights 500 to 900, upright and italic. It's licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org), which allows bundling it with the game. It stands in for Serie A's
own lettering, which isn't freely licensed (see `styles.css`).

`archivo-latin.woff2` and `archivo-latin-italic.woff2` are Archivo, by Omnibus-Type, from Google
Fonts: the Latin subset of its variable font, every width (62% to 125%) and weight (100 to 900),
upright and italic. It's licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 too. It's the 1990 look's
lettering (see `italia90.css`): drawn wide for titles and buttons, at its normal width for the rest.
