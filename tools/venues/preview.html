Venue preview

Every venue as the game draws it on a phone held upright, from the game's own camera, a few seconds into free play with things in flight, its twist going and the player heading every ball. Options in the address: ?venue=park,moon for some venues only, ?at=12000 for how far into the round, in milliseconds (9,000 by default), ?size=844x390 for another screen, in CSS pixels (390×844 by default), ?kit=rossoneri, ?seed=7, and ?placeholder for the stand-in player the game draws without src/game/models/player.glb. ?quality=low (or medium) draws them as a phone on that tier would (see src/game/quality.ts). ?idle leaves the player standing still in the middle, to be knocked out: with a late enough ?at, the picture's of them down and the camera eased back. Each caption says how many draw calls its frame took, shadows and all, and how long the venue took to build (in a browser without a GPU, mostly the time it takes to bake its lighting).
