Venue backdrops

Paints each venue's backdrop: its sky and everything round the pitch that stays put, built in 3D from the venue's own scenery and pictured from where the game's camera stands, then saved as <venue>.webp. Move the files into src/game/backdrops/. Each picture holds three parts of the backdrop's card side by side: the middle, finely, and either side of it, more coarsely (see src/game/backdrop.ts). Options in the address: ?venue=park,moon for some venues only, and ?quality=0.8 for the WebP quality to start from, which is lowered until a picture's under 200 KB.
