Build the venues' Meshy props

Makes each venue's Meshy model in tools/props/in/ (a GLB, as Meshy downloads it, renamed to the prop's name below) into a prop-<name>.glb for the game, and each of the crowds' Meshy figures that stand still, in tools/crowd/in/, into a crowd-<name>.glb, then downloads them. ?only=park-tree,penguin builds just those. See ASSETS.md.

Each prop stands on the origin, centred and 1 m tall, since the venues say how tall each one stands. Its pictures are capped at 512 px, as fine as anything that far down the pitch shows, and only the props that should glow keep Meshy's glow map.
