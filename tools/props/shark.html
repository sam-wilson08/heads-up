Build the shark

Makes the Underwater venue's shark, which swims across the pitch (see src/game/twists/underwater.ts), from Meshy's FBX download: put the .fbx and its four pictures in tools/props/in/shark/ as shark.fbx, colour.png, normal.png, roughness.png and metallic.png. It's made into prop-shark.glb and downloaded: move it to src/game/models/. See ASSETS.md.

The shark lies along +X, nose first, 1 m long and centred on the origin, since the venue says how long it is. Its pictures are capped at 512 px, and it's simplified if it has more than 8,000 triangles. The pictures below show it from the side and from above, nose to the right, to check it's the right way round.
