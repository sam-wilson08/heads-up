Build the dragon

Makes Sakura Peak's dragon, which flies its laps of the sky (see src/game/twists/dragon.ts), from Meshy's GLB download: put it in tools/props/in/dragon/ as dragon.glb. It's made into prop-dragon.glb and downloaded: move it to src/game/models/. See ASSETS.md.

The dragon lies dead straight along +X, head first, its back up +Y, 1 m long and centred on the origin: the game bends it along its flight, so Meshy's must be straight too. Its colour's capped at 2048 px, since Meshy's remesh cuts it into small pieces whose edges blur at less, and its normal map at 1024 px, both as JPEG; its metal and roughness map is left out, since it's evenly matte and no metal. It's simplified if it has more than 24,000 triangles. The pictures below show it from the side and from above, head to the right, to check it's the right way round.
