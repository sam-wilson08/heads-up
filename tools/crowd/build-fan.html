Build fan.glb

Combines the fan Meshy rigged and animated, and its textures, from tools/crowd/in/fan/ into one fan.glb for the crowd, then downloads it: fan.fbx (the model with its cheer, from Meshy's animation download, With Skin, at 30 fps), and basecolor.png, normal.png, roughness.png and metallic.png. See ASSETS.md. ?figure=fan-woman builds another fan the same way, from tools/crowd/in/fan-woman/fan-woman.fbx into fan-woman.glb, and ?figure=alien another crowd's rigged figure, from tools/crowd/in/alien/alien.fbx into crowd-alien.glb.

The fan is 1.75 m tall, standing on the origin and facing +Z, as the crowd's figures do. Its fingers' bones go, their skin moving with the hands instead, since no one sees a fan's fingers from the pitch and each bone is work every frame for every fan. Its pictures are capped at 512 px, as fine as a fan ever shows, and its clip is called cheer.
