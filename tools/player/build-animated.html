Build the animated player.glb

Combines the player as Mixamo rigged it, with its idle, walk, jump, crouch and fall, and Meshy's textures into one player.glb, then downloads it. Put these in tools/player/in/: Mixamo's idle.fbx (Ready Idle), jump.fbx, walk.fbx, crouch.fbx (Standing To Crouched, or Crouched To Standing), fall.fbx (Falling Down) and header.fbx (Headbutt, or Illegal Headbutt), each FBX Binary, With Skin, at 30 fps, and Meshy's basecolor.png, normal.png, roughness.png and metallic.png. The player comes from idle.fbx; the others only need the same Mixamo skeleton, so a clip downloaded for an earlier player still plays on it.

The model is in the game's metres: 1.8 m tall, standing on the origin, facing +Z as Meshy's does. Its clips are in place, since the game moves the player itself: idle (the idle, looped while the player stands still, with the feet kept where they stand); step_left and step_right (the walk, and the walk mirrored); jump (just the time in the air, with the lowest foot kept on the ground, for the game to lift) and jump_mirrored (the same, mirrored, which the game takes turns with so no two jumps in a row look alike); header (the headbutt, from just before the head is furthest forward until it's back, with the feet kept where they stand, for the game to play as the ball's headed); duck (the crouch, from standing down into it, played backwards if Mixamo's comes up out of it, with the feet kept where they stand); and fall_right and fall_left (the fall, back and to that side, and the fall mirrored), which travel, since the player ends up lying where they land.
